Features

Secure by default, minimal by design.

Every document gets a unique hash, decentralized storage, and an optional AI‑computed trust score—without cluttering your workflow.

◎

Blockchain fingerprint

Each credential is hashed and anchored on‑chain, giving you a verifiable fingerprint that cannot be forged or silently edited.

↻

AI trust scoring

Lightweight vision models compare signatures and layouts to known references, returning a simple 0–100 trust score.

▢

QR‑first verification

Each PDF includes a compact QR code linking to the canonical record. Scan on any phone, see authenticity in seconds.
